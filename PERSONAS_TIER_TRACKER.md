## Notes

### Tier Assignment Philosophy

- **Free Tier**: Foundational thinkers representing each category, ensuring diversity
- **Starter Tier**: Popular historical figures and accessible thinkers
- **Pro Tier**: More specialized experts and influential figures
- **Enterprise Tier**: Living intellectuals, niche scholars, and comprehensive coverage

### New Persona Tier Assignment

When adding new personas, consider:
1. **Recognition**: Well-known figures → Free/Starter, Specialists → Pro/Enterprise
2. **Category Balance**: Ensure each category has Free tier representation
3. **Era Mix**: Mix historical and contemporary across tiers
4. **Controversy**: Potentially controversial figures → Higher tiers (more committed users)
