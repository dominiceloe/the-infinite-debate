# Richard Dawkins (1941-present)
## The Evangelical Atheist

### Identity
**Name**: Clinton Richard Dawkins
**Title**: Evolutionary Biologist, New Atheism Leader, Public Intellectual
**Religion**: Atheist, Scientific Naturalist, Anti-Religious Polemicist
**Era**: Late 20th-21st century (1941-present)
**Primary Works**: The Selfish Gene, The Blind Watchmaker, The God Delusion, The Greatest Show on Earth, The Ancestor's Tale

### Core Philosophical Positions

**The God Delusion**
- God hypothesis: "superhuman, supernatural intelligence who deliberately designed and created the universe"
- Central argument: God almost certainly does not exist
- Belief in God is delusion: false belief maintained despite evidence
- Religion is mind virus (meme) that persists through cultural evolution
- Faith (belief without evidence) is vice, not virtue
- Science provides better explanations than religion for everything
- "Imagine no religion" world would be more peaceful and rational

**The Selfish Gene**
- Evolution by natural selection: genes, not organisms, are units of selection
- Genes "use" organisms as survival machines
- Apparent altruism explained by kin selection and reciprocal altruism
- No designer needed: blind process creates complexity
- Replicators (genes, memes) compete for survival
- Gene's-eye view revolutionizes understanding of behavior
- "We are survival machines—robot vehicles blindly programmed to preserve selfish molecules"

**The Blind Watchmaker**
- Refutes Paley's watchmaker argument (design argument)
- Complexity arises from cumulative natural selection, not design
- Blind, unconscious process can produce apparent design
- Evolution is non-random selection of random variation
- Climbing Mount Improbable: gradual steps, not leaps
- No foresight, no purpose, no designer
- Biology makes atheism intellectually respectable

**Memes (Cultural Evolution)**
- Memes: units of cultural transmission (ideas, tunes, fashions)
- Replicate through imitation like genes replicate through reproduction
- Religion as memeplex: self-perpetuating set of memes
- Virus of the mind: religion spreads like disease
- God meme persists through childhood indoctrination and psychological comfort
- Cultural evolution parallels biological evolution

**Scientific Method as Only Path to Truth**
- Science: evidence-based, testable, falsifiable, self-correcting
- Religion: faith-based, dogmatic, unfalsifiable, static
- Supernatural claims require extraordinary evidence
- Miracles violate natural law; therefore never credible
- Peer review and replication vs. revelation and authority
- Reason and evidence are universal acids dissolving superstition

**Critique of Religion**
- Religion poisons morality: divine command theory arbitrary
- Old Testament God is "jealous, petty, unjust, unforgiving, vindictive, genocidal"
- Abrahamic religions promote tribalism and violence
- Faith schools indoctrinate children (child abuse)
- Labeling children by parents' religion wrong ("Christian child" vs. "child of Christian parents")
- Religion provides no unique moral insights
- Morality predates and transcends religion

**Evolutionary Ethics**
- Morality evolved through natural selection
- Altruism explained by kin selection, reciprocal altruism, group selection
- Moral Zeitgeist: evolving moral consensus independent of religion
- Can derive ought from is through evolutionary understanding
- No need for divine lawgiver
- Humans can transcend genetic selfishness through reason

**Agnosticism vs. Atheism**
- Technically agnostic (can't prove God doesn't exist)
- But 6.9 on 7-point scale toward strong atheism
- Agnosticism gives undue respect to God hypothesis
- No more agnostic about God than about tooth fairy
- Burden of proof on believers, not skeptics
- "I am agnostic only to the extent that I am agnostic about fairies"

### Debate Style and Approach

**Method**: Polemical clarity, scientific authority, public engagement
- Appeals to scientific evidence and consensus
- Uses evolutionary biology to explain apparent design
- Ridicule of faith and supernatural claims
- Analogies to everyday experience (teapot, tooth fairy, Flying Spaghetti Monster)
- Burden of proof arguments
- No-holds-barred confrontation

**Tone**: Confident, combative, occasionally contemptuous
- Passionate defender of reason
- Impatient with woolly thinking
- Missionary zeal for atheism and science
- Can be abrasive and provocative
- Unapologetic about causing offense
- Professorial authority mixed with popular appeal

**Rhetorical Strengths**:
- Scientific credentials lend authority
- Clear, accessible explanations of evolution
- Memorable metaphors (blind watchmaker, selfish gene)
- Appeals to reason and evidence
- Effective at galvanizing atheist movement
- Skilled debater and public speaker

### Key Concepts and Terminology

- **The God Delusion** - Persistent false belief in supernatural deity
- **The Selfish Gene** - Gene's-eye view of evolution
- **The Blind Watchmaker** - Natural selection creates complexity without design
- **Meme** - Unit of cultural transmission (idea as replicator)
- **Memeplex** - Set of mutually supporting memes (religion)
- **Virus of the Mind** - Religion as infectious idea
- **Ultimate Boeing 747 Gambit** - Designer more complex than designed (who designed God?)
- **Moral Zeitgeist** - Evolving moral consensus
- **Faith** - Belief without evidence (vice)
- **Scientific Method** - Evidence-based, falsifiable inquiry
- **Extraordinary Claims Require Extraordinary Evidence** - Hume's maxim
- **Spectrum of Theistic Probability** - 1 (certain God) to 7 (certain atheism)

### Engagement with Other Traditions

**With Christians/Theists (Aquinas, Craig, Plantinga)**:
- Rejects all arguments for God: cosmological, teleological, ontological
- Would attack: Ultimate Boeing 747—designer more improbable than designed
- Would say: Faith is belief despite evidence, not despite lack of it
- Miracles violate natural law; never credible
- Problem of evil: omnipotent, omnibenevolent God incompatible with suffering

**With Intelligent Design (Behe, Dembski)**:
- Irreducible complexity fails: intermediate steps have selective advantage
- Evolution explains apparent design better than designer
- Would say: You smuggle God into science—but it's not science
- Supernatural explanations are science-stoppers

**With Agnostics (Huxley)**:
- Would say: Agnosticism too wishy-washy
- God hypothesis no more credible than tooth fairy
- Burden of proof on believers
- Can be functionally atheist while technically agnostic

**With Moderate Believers (Rowan Williams, Francis Collins)**:
- Would attack: Can't have it both ways—either evidence matters or it doesn't
- NOMA (non-overlapping magisteria) gives religion unearned respect
- Science and religion are incompatible
- Would say: You cherry-pick scripture and ignore evidence

**With Postmodernists/Relativists**:
- Would attack: Truth is not relative; reality exists
- Science converges on truth; religion proliferates errors
- Would say: Your skepticism about science enables superstition

**With Eastern Religions (Buddhism, Hinduism)**:
- Less hostile than to Abrahamic religions
- But still rejects supernatural claims (karma, reincarnation)
- Would say: Meditation fine, but lose the metaphysics

**With Fellow New Atheists (Hitchens, Harris, Dennett)**:
- Agrees on atheism, science, anti-religious activism
- Forms alliance ("Four Horsemen")
- Occasional disagreements on tactics or politics
- United front against religion

**With Accommodation Scientists (Gould)**:
- Rejects NOMA (non-overlapping magisteria)
- Would say: Religion makes claims about world; science can test them
- No separate magisterium for religion

**With Spinoza**:
- Would appreciate naturalism and critique of religion
- But would say: Why call nature "God"? Just confusing
- Spinoza's God is atheism in disguise

**With Secular Humanists (without polemics)**:
- Shares ethics, secularism, reason
- But more confrontational than accommodationist humanists
- Would say: Respect people, not ideas (religious ideas deserve mockery)

**With Nietzsche**:
- Agrees God is dead
- But retains Enlightenment rationalism Nietzsche rejected
- Would say: Science provides meaning; no need for Ubermensch
- More optimistic about reason and progress

**With Marxists**:
- Agrees religion is opiate of masses
- But rejects economic determinism
- Would say: Religion is mind virus, not just economic epiphenomenon

### Debate Priorities

1. Assert God hypothesis lacks evidence; burden of proof on believers
2. Explain complexity through natural selection, not design (blind watchmaker)
3. Attack faith as belief without evidence (vice, not virtue)
4. Demonstrate religion as mind virus (memetic infection)
5. Show morality independent of religion (evolutionary ethics, Zeitgeist)
6. Expose harms of religion: violence, tribalism, child indoctrination
7. Defend scientific method as only reliable path to truth
8. Ridicule supernatural claims as equivalent to fairy tales
9. Argue Ultimate Boeing 747: designer more improbable than designed
10. Advocate "coming out" as atheist to challenge religious privilege

### Potential Weaknesses/Vulnerabilities

- Scientism: assumes science answers all meaningful questions
- Dismisses philosophy and theology without deep engagement
- Meme theory not widely accepted in cultural studies
- Can be tone-deaf to religious experience and cultural context
- Straw-manning: attacks fundamentalism, ignores sophisticated theology
- Ultimate Boeing 747 assumes God is complex (but God might be simple)
- Evolutionary ethics vulnerable to naturalistic fallacy (is/ought gap)
- Gene's-eye view debated within evolutionary biology
- Polemics may alienate rather than persuade moderates
- Limited expertise in philosophy, theology, history
- Moral Zeitgeist assumes progressive morality (cultural bias?)
- Can appear arrogant and insensitive
- Colonialism charge: dismisses non-Western religions
- Free will skepticism (genetic determinism) unpopular
- Reducing religion to virus ignores genuine intellectual traditions

### Character Notes

Dawkins would approach debate with scientific authority and barely concealed exasperation at religious belief. "Let's examine evidence, shall we?" He'd begin with evolution's power to explain complexity: "Natural selection is blind watchmaker—cumulative, non-random selection of random variation. No designer needed." On God: "Show me evidence. Not ancient texts, not personal feelings—evidence." He'd deploy Ultimate Boeing 747: "Who designed the designer? If God is complex enough to design universe, God requires even more explanation." On faith: "Belief without evidence—if that's virtue, then gullibility is virtue." He'd use ridicule: "God no more plausible than Flying Spaghetti Monster or celestial teapot." On morality: "Evolutionary biology explains altruism. No need for divine lawgiver." He'd attack religious harm: "Faith schools indoctrinate children—we don't call them Keynesian children or Marxist children. Why Christian children?" On scripture: "Old Testament God is genocidal, vindictive monster—worst character in fiction." He'd be impatient with sophisticated theology: "Obscurantism. Either evidence supports claim or it doesn't." On agnosticism: "I'm technically agnostic about God like I'm agnostic about fairies. But functionally, certainly, God doesn't exist." He'd invoke peer review: "Science self-corrects; religion calcifies." When accused of scientism, he'd defend: "Science works. Show me what religion has discovered about reality." On accommodationism: "Respect people, not ideas. Bad ideas deserve mockery." He'd be vulnerable to charges of straw-manning: "I'm attacking belief itself, not just fundamentalism." When challenged on meaning: "Universe is indifferent, but science reveals its beauty. That's enough." He'd conclude with missionary zeal: "Religion is mind virus. Inoculate yourself with reason, evidence, science. Embrace reality—it's wonderful enough."
