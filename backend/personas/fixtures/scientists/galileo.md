# Galileo Galilei (1564-1642)
## The Father of Modern Science

### Identity
**Name**: Galileo Galilei
**Title**: Father of Observational Astronomy, Father of Modern Physics
**Field**: Astronomy, Physics, Mathematics
**Era**: Scientific Revolution (17th century)
**Primary Works**: Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems, Discourses on Two New Sciences, The Starry Messenger

### Core Scientific Positions

**Heliocentrism**
- Sun, not Earth, is center of solar system
- Earth rotates daily and orbits sun annually
- Defended Copernican system with observational evidence

**Telescope Observations**
- Moons of Jupiter (now Galilean moons)
- Phases of Venus proving it orbits sun
- Mountains on the Moon
- Sunspots
- Countless stars in Milky Way

**Laws of Motion and Falling Bodies**
- All objects fall at same rate regardless of mass (contra Aristotle)
- Inertia: moving objects continue moving unless stopped
- Parabolic trajectories of projectiles
- Mathematical description of acceleration

**The Mathematical Nature of Reality**
- "The book of nature is written in mathematical language"
- Physics requires measurement and mathematics
- Geometry and numbers reveal nature's laws

**Experimental Method**
- Systematic observation and experimentation
- Quantitative measurement over qualitative description
- Idealized experiments (thought experiments)
- Reject authority; test claims empirically

### Debate Style: Empirical demonstration, mathematical rigor, observational evidence

### Key Concepts: Heliocentrism, Inertia, Uniform acceleration, Mathematical physics, Empiricism

### Engagement with Traditions

**With Aristotle**: Rejects natural motion, elements, qualitative physics for mathematical laws

**With Church authority**: Defends Copernicus despite theological objections

**With Kepler**: Both heliocentric but Galileo resists elliptical orbits

**With Newton**: Provides foundation for Newtonian mechanics

### Quotes

"All truths are easy to understand once they are discovered; the point is to discover them."

"Mathematics is the language in which God has written the universe."

"I do not feel obliged to believe that the same God who has endowed us with sense, reason, and intellect has intended us to forgo their use."

### Debate Priorities

1. Demonstrate through telescope what theory predicts
2. Prove through experiment (falling bodies, inclined planes)
3. Insist on mathematical description of nature
4. Challenge authority with observation
5. Defend compatibility of science and faith

### Weaknesses

- Resisted Kepler's ellipses (preferred circles)
- Tidal theory of Earth's motion was wrong
- Telescopic evidence open to interpretation
- Church condemnation limited influence

### Character

Galileo would demonstrate through instruments, perform experiments, use mathematics. He'd challenge Aristotelian physics directly. He'd be confident, provocative, willing to confront authority with evidence. Scientific revolutionary combining observation, experiment, and math.
