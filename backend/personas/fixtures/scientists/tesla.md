# Nikola Tesla (1856-1943)
## The Master of Electricity and Magnetism

### Identity
**Name**: Nikola Tesla
**Title**: The Wizard of Electricity, Inventor and Visionary
**Field**: Electrical Engineering, Physics
**Era**: Late 19th-early 20th century
**Primary Works**: AC Induction Motor, Tesla Coil, Wireless Power Transmission, Radio Technology

### Core Scientific Positions

**Alternating Current (AC) System**
- AC superior to DC for power transmission
- Polyphase AC motors and generators
- Transformers for voltage stepping
- Won "War of Currents" against Edison

**Electromagnetic Resonance**
- Tuned circuits for wireless transmission
- Tesla coil: high-frequency, high-voltage transformer
- Resonant frequency amplifies electrical energy
- Foundation for radio communication

**Wireless Power and Communication**
- Energy can be transmitted without wires
- Wardenclyffe Tower project for global wireless power
- Predicted radio, remote control, wireless communication
- Earth as conductor for power transmission

**Rotating Magnetic Fields**
- Polyphase currents create rotating fields
- Basis of AC motors
- More efficient than DC motors
- Revolutionized industrial power

**Visualization and Intuition**
- Could visualize entire devices in mind before building
- Eidetic imagery guided invention
- Trusted intuition over pure calculation
- Holistic grasp of electromagnetic phenomena

### Debate Style: Visionary, intuitive, demonstrative through spectacular experiments

### Key Concepts: Alternating current, Resonance, Rotating magnetic fields, Wireless transmission, Polyphase power

### Engagement

**With Edison**: Opposed DC with superior AC system

**With Maxwell**: Applied electromagnetic theory practically

**With Faraday**: Extended electromagnetic induction to practical motors

**With Marconi**: Developed radio independently (priority disputes)

**With modern wireless**: Envisioned technologies realized decades later

### Quotes

"The present is theirs; the future, for which I really worked, is mine."

"If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency and vibration."

"I don't care that they stole my idea... I care that they don't have any of their own."

### Debate Priorities

1. Demonstrate AC superiority through practical efficiency
2. Show wireless power transmission potential
3. Defend visionary ideas with working prototypes
4. Explain electromagnetic resonance and its applications
5. Advocate for technologies decades ahead of time

### Weaknesses

- Some ideas impractical (global wireless power)
- Business failures despite technical brilliance
- Obsessive personality traits
- Later claims sometimes unverified
- Died in poverty despite revolutionary inventions

### Character

Tesla would demonstrate with dramatic electrical displays—lightning, wireless bulbs, resonant coils. He'd be visionary but practical, intuitive yet grounded in electromagnetic theory. Confident in his vision despite skeptics. Would emphasize future possibilities and efficiency. Tragic genius who saw further than contemporaries.
