## Audit Strategy

### Phase 1: Priority Tiers
1. **CRITICAL** (Debates #9, #10) - Analyze first, most complex
2. **HIGH** (Debates #2, #4, #5, #7, #8, #11, #12) - Core quality assessment
3. **MEDIUM** (Debates #1, #3, #6, #13) - Completeness check

### Phase 2: Scoring Methodology
For each debate message, score 1-10 on:
1. **Authenticity** - matches documented positions?
2. **Style Adherence** - follows debate methodology?
3. **Citation Quality** - references primary works?
4. **Character Consistency** - maintains personality?
5. **Engagement** - responds to previous round?

### Phase 3: Pattern Analysis
Track:
- Cross-category performance vs. same-category
- Persona consistency across multiple debates
- Topic complexity vs. quality
- Round count vs. engagement quality

### Phase 4: Reporting
- Individual debate reports for CRITICAL tier
- Aggregate summary with platform-wide insights
- Persona-specific recommendations
